XCloak Security Suite

XCloak Recon

AI-orchestrated penetration testing. Describe a goal, the AI plans and runs nmap, nuclei, gobuster, sqlmap, nikto, ffuf, and whatweb, then synthesizes the results into a risk-scored report.

  • · AI planning (GPT-4o, Claude, or local Ollama)
  • · Live WebSocket scan streaming
  • · Human approval before follow-up actions
  • · PDF reports, findings DB, scan history
