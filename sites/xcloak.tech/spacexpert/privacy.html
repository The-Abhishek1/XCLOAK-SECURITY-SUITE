Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 22, 2026

SpaceXpert ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the SpaceXpert mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

SpaceXpert is designed as a local-first utility. Most data processing occurs directly on your device to ensure maximum privacy and speed.

  • File Metadata: To provide storage analysis and duplicate detection, the app scans file names, sizes, and timestamps. This data never leaves your device.
  • Biometric Data: The "Quantum Vault" uses Android's system-level biometric prompts. We do not have access to, nor do we store, your actual fingerprint or facial data.

2. Permissions and Data Access

To function correctly, SpaceXpert requires specific sensitive permissions:

  • All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): This is required for the core functionality of the app: identifying junk files, finding duplicates, and managing large files across your entire storage.
  • Media Access: Used to display and analyze photos and videos for the "Similar Photo Finder" feature.

3. Data Security (The Vault)

Files moved to the "Quantum Vault" are encrypted using AES-256 bit encryption. The encryption keys are managed locally via the Android Keystore system. We cannot recover your files if you lose access to your device's security credentials.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use the following third-party services which may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services: For app updates and licensing.
  • AdMob: For displaying non-intrusive advertisements (in the free version). AdMob may use device identifiers to personalize ads.
  • Google Play Billing: To process Pro subscriptions securely via Google. We do not see or store your credit card information.

5. Data Retention

SpaceXpert does not operate an account system. We do not store your data on our servers. Any "Trash" data is stored locally on your device and is automatically purged after 7 days by the app's internal logic.

6. Your Rights

Since we do not collect your data on our servers, you have full control over your information by managing the app locally or uninstalling the application.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
