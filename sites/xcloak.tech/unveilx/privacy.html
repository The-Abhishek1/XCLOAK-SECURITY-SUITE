Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 30, 2026

At X-Cloak Security, your privacy is not just a feature—it is our entire mission. This Privacy Policy describes how UnveilX handles information on your Android device.

1. Local-First Architecture

UnveilX is designed with a Local-First philosophy. This means that the vast majority of your data never leaves your device. We do not maintain central servers to store your personal activity logs, passwords, or network history.

2. Data Collection and Usage

  • Sensor & Activity Data: UnveilX monitors Microphone, Camera, and Location usage. This data is stored in a local encrypted database on your device and is used solely to provide you with the Activity Timeline.
  • Network Traffic: When the Traffic Monitor is active, UnveilX uses a local VPN tunnel to analyze domain requests. All analysis happens on-device. No traffic data is uploaded to our servers.
  • Password Auditor: Your credentials are encrypted using the Android Keystore and stored locally. For breach checks, we use k-Anonymity (sending only the first 5 characters of a SHA-1 hash) to check against public databases without ever exposing your actual password.

3. Third-Party Services

To support the development of UnveilX, we use the following services:

  • Google AdMob: Used in the free version to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.
  • Google Play Billing: Used to process the "UnveilX Pro" upgrade. Payment details are handled securely by Google and never shared with us.

4. Data Retention

You have full control over your data. You can configure data retention periods (7, 30, 90 days) or manually clear all history within the app settings at any time.

5. Permissions

UnveilX requires specific permissions (Usage Stats, Notifications) to function correctly. These are used only to map system events to the apps that triggered them and to provide real-time alerts.
